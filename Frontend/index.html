Stage 01: Satellite Imagery

            
ARCHIVE INGESTION

            

              Ingests raw Top-Of-Atmosphere (TOA L1C) and Bottom-Of-Atmosphere (BOA L2A) granules from Copernicus Sentinel-2 (MSI), Sentinel-1 (C-SAR), Landsat Collection 2 (OLI), and Bhuvan/NRSC datasets.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
Core Computational Principles

            
The algorithmic foundation of semantic Earth-observation retrieval.

          

          

            

              

                
                Embeddings
              

              

                Multimodal spatial-spectral representations that map multi-spectral satellite chips and natural-language text queries into a shared high-dimensional vector space.
              

            

            

              

                
                Vector Search
              

              

                Sub-second approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) retrieval over multi-terabyte indexes, returning imagery whose semantic representations are closest to the analyst query.
              

            

            

              

                
                Multi-Temporal Analysis
              

              

                Multi-epoch comparisons evaluating spectral indices (NDBI, MNDWI, NDVI) against historic seasonal baselines to isolate genuine anthropogenic developments from natural changes.
              

            

            

              

                
                Provenance
              

              

                Cryptographic audit trail recording source satellite granule IDs, acquisition timestamps, sensor parameters, processing version, and analyst review status.

            Current Engineering Limitations & Operational Boundaries
          

          
    
            
  • Large geographic archives require significant local GPU vector caching and index memory.
    • 
            
  • Severe cloud overcast conditions in optical imagery require synthetic aperture radar (SAR) fallback data.
    • 
            
  • Sensor ground sampling distance (10m) sets the lower threshold for structural footprint resolution.
    • 
            
  • Extreme seasonal monsoon inundation can introduce complex hydrologic spectral variances requiring multi-year normalization.
    • 
            
  • AETHER is engineered as an analyst-support decision system; final operational determinations require qualified human verification.
